Privacy-First Features

Everything runs on your machine.

No cloud. No accounts. No subscriptions. Just you, your voice, and your Windows PC.

🔒

Zero Network Calls

Audio is processed 100% on-device. Nothing is ever transmitted to any server. Your voice data stays on your PC. Always.

🗑️

No Audio Storage

Audio buffers are discarded immediately after transcription. Nothing is saved to disk. Ever. Your conversations stay yours.

📖

Fully Open Source

Every line of code is public on GitHub. Audit it yourself. No hidden anything. Transparent by default.

📡

No Telemetry

No analytics. No crash reporting. No usage tracking. We literally cannot see what you do. Your privacy is absolute.

⌨️

System-Wide Text Injection

Transcribed text appears wherever your cursor is. Browsers, Slack, VS Code, Word, Excel, terminals. Everywhere.

🎯

Push-to-Talk & Toggle

Hold a hotkey to record or double-tap to toggle. Simple, predictable, works exactly the way you expect.

🌍

99+ Languages

Auto-detect or specify your language. English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, and many more supported.

⚡

GPU Accelerated

Leverages NVIDIA CUDA, AMD, and Intel GPU acceleration for blazing fast transcription on Windows hardware.

⚙️

Fully Configurable

Choose hotkeys, models, languages, silence detection thresholds. A settings panel that puts you in full control.

How It Works

Three steps. That's it.

No accounts. No sign-ups. No cloud. Just install and speak.

1

Press Your Hotkey

Press and hold your chosen hotkey. The system tray icon lights up. You're recording.

2

Speak

Say whatever you want. Your audio is processed entirely on your PC. Nothing leaves your machine.

3

Release

Text appears at your cursor. In any app. Browser, Slack, VS Code, Word. Anywhere on Windows.
